# Northwind examples

The [phase-one baseline](../northwind-phase-one/README.md) preserves the complete
read-only source projection. Its frozen artifact and source identities remain
separate from phase-two authoring.

[Business provenance](business/provenance.md) records which legacy assumptions
came from source computations and which were introduced by an agent. Source SQL
and its results belong to phase one; interpreting or changing their business
meaning belongs to phase two.

The [phase-two pilot](business/README.md) keeps supplied evidence and model deductions visibly distinct.
Public documents use English; the shared reader also supports Chinese customer
review. Supplied does not mean approved, and inferred does not mean incorrect.
